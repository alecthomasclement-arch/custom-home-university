An independent architect's second set of eyes

Before you approve your custom home plans, know what you're saying yes to.

You're about to invest a significant amount of money in a home designed around your life. But a beautiful set of drawings doesn't always make it easy to see how the finished spaces will feel, function, or fit your priorities.

Before you sign off, can you confidently answer:

  • Will the layout work for the way your family actually lives?
  • Are there design decisions that could improve the home or simplify potential construction costs?
  • What should you ask your builder or architect before changes become harder to make?
